#### A few things I've made
- **[LiveDepthField](https://decodedxr.github.io/LiveDepthField/)** - turn a webcam or photo into an explorable 3D point cloud, entirely in the browser. **Try it:** open [the live site](https://decodedxr.github.io/LiveDepthField/), allow your camera or pick a photo, then drag to orbit the scene. Code on [GitHub](https://github.com/DecodedXR/LiveDepthField).
- **[Aurora · Lofi Focus Station](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aurora-lofi-focus-station/id6796833999)** - a gamified focus app: Pomodoro timer + lofi music and cozy animated scenes, with streaks, an embers economy, and journaling. **Get it:** free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aurora-lofi-focus-station/id6796833999) for iPhone and iPad.
- **[SoloLeveling](https://github.com/DecodedXR/SoloLeveling)** *(work in progress)* - an iOS fitness RPG where the camera is the referee and XP can't be faked. **Try it:** not on the App Store yet; clone [the repo](https://github.com/DecodedXR/SoloLeveling), open `ios/SoloApp` in Xcode, and run it on your iPhone.
- **DecoBot** - a Discord bot for my friends' server that turns everyone's class schedules into pingable class roles, running 24/7 on Google Cloud. **Use it:** post your Purdue schedule CSV in `#schedules`, react in `#class-roles` to join classes, then try `/free`, `/exams`, and `/leaderboard`. Private to our server.
